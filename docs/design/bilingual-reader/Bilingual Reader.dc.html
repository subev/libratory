Bilingual reading

Recommended: a Bilingual toggle inside the existing reader that keeps your view and adds the translation. In Column, the page crop stays and the translation runs in a lane beside it. In Text, both are reflowed as paired passages. Rows are aligned by passage. Clicking a word inspects it; listening is always an explicit action. On phones, passages stack in reading order with the transport docked at the bottom. The chapter modal gets one contextual link; the embedded preview is deferred.

  • Column view (default here): the original stays the real page crop. The translation is reflowed in a lane beside it, one passage per paragraph of the crop. Crops are split only at paragraph boundaries from the layout, never mid-line or scaled. When a translation runs longer, blank paper opens under that paragraph so the next pair lines up.
  • On the crop: the existing cue rects mark the active sentence and spoken word. Clicking a word rect inspects it. Pages without a text layer mark the paragraph only, and words there can't be inspected. The translation lane still works.
  • Rows are passages. Each paragraph pair shares a grid row, so a counterpart is always beside its original. Unequal lengths just make the row taller. No scroll-percentage sync.
  • Original left, translation right, whatever the direction. Hebrew is RTL inside its column only; headings, bar and time stay LTR.
  • Playback: the active sentence pair is tinted on both sides. Spoken word = filled. Counterpart = outlined. “Speaking” names the voice's language in words.
  • Follow: when the active row leaves the band (120px top, 140px bottom), the view scrolls to it. Scrolling by hand pauses following, but playback keeps going, and “Back to the … voice” appears. Pressing it, or Listen from here, resumes following.
  • Click a word = inspect (neutral underline, no seek). Dragging selects text as usual. See 04.
  • Leaving: Bilingual off → the same view in one language, at the same sentence. Column ↔ Text keeps the pairing and the passage.
  • Try tweaks: Bulgarian–German sample, sentence timing, dark.

Gesture change, deliberately: the ordinary reader seeks when you tap a passage. In bilingual mode that tap inspects instead, because word lookup is the reason to be there, and seeking by accident loses your place in the voice. Seeking moves to Listen from here. Single-language Text, Column and Page keep tap-to-seek. Inspection is neutral (tinted fill + underline). Playback is accent. Both can show at once without being confused.

Tooltip: {{ m.title }}
  • Placement: right after the variant pills, the reading controls, and away from Re-synthesize and Translate.
  • Carries: chapter, the selected translation (the Hebrew pill, or the book's last-used translation from Original), and the passage at the modal's playhead if it has a valid anchor. Opens /books/:id/read?chapter=3&with=he&at=s4.
  • Playback: modal narration pauses before navigation. The reader opens paused at that passage, in the voice that was playing.
  • Editing: disabled, “Save or discard your edits first.”
  • Missing translation: disabled, “No translation of this chapter yet.” Rewrites like Plain English don't count.
  • Compare stays as it is: editing and review of a variant. Its tooltip now says “review and edit” to separate it from reading.
  • Embedded preview: not recommended. The modal already has its own transport, pills and text views. A second language toolbar would crowd it. Revisit after the reader ships.
